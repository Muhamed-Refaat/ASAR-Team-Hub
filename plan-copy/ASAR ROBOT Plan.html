1. System Evolution & Heterogeneous Architecture

Transitioning from a basic 115200 baud ASCII serial setup to a robust three-tier heterogeneous processing model resolves severe hardware bottlenecks. High-dimensional LiDAR point clouds, camera visual odometry, and real-time trajectory optimization are offloaded to a POSIX Linux Single-Board Computer (Raspberry Pi), leaving microcontrollers free for deterministic interrupt handling.

Extended Kalman Filter State Fusion

Raw wheel encoders drift due to mechanical wheel slip, while IMU accelerometers suffer from motor vibration noise. Discrete EKF balances prediction and measurement covariance matrices.

1. Non-Linear State Prediction x̂_k⁻ = f(x̂_{k-1}, u_k) & P_k⁻ = F_k P_{k-1} F_kᵀ + Q_k
2. Kalman Gain Update K_k = P_k⁻ H_kᵀ (H_k P_k⁻ H_kᵀ + R_k)⁻¹
3. State & Uncertainty Correction x̂_k = x̂_k⁻ + K_k (z_k - h(x̂_k⁻))

🔋 Hardware & Power Isolation

6. Custom Safety PCB & Power Isolation Blueprint

Directly driving high-current DC gear motors alongside sensitive high-speed digital logic causes voltage droops and inductive back-EMF spikes. A custom 2-layer KiCad PCB isolates logic, driver gate, and high-power rails.

Power Distribution Current Allocation (Amperes)

Power Rails & Protection Architecture

5.0V / 3A Logic Rail
DC-DC Buck Regulator
Powers Raspberry Pi 5, ESP32, Arduino Mega & LiDAR.
9.0V / 2A Gate Rail
Isolated Buck Regulator
Supplies logic gate inputs for motor H-Bridge drivers.
12.6V Raw Rail
Direct 3S LiPo Supply
Feeds full current to motor driver high-power MOSFETs.

Circuit Safety Protections

  • Battery BMS: 3S 20A BMS with active balancing, UVLO (<2.7V/cell), and over-voltage cutoff.
  • Overcurrent & Reverse Polarity: 15A Automotive Blade Fuse + SUP90P06 P-MOSFET.
  • Optocoupler Signal Isolation: PC817 / 6N137 optocouplers isolating MCU PWM from drivers.
  • Transient Suppression: TVS Diodes + 1000µF bulk decoupling capacitors on power inputs.

KiCad PCB Design Rules & Star Grounding Topology

Trace Sizing & Copper Weight 2 oz/ft² copper weight. High-current motor supply traces set to 3.0mm – 5.0mm width. Signal logic traces standard 0.25mm - 0.35mm.
Ground Plane Partitioning Preserve bottom layer for ground plane. Physically separate Analog GND, Digital GND, and Power GND.
Star Ground Connection Connect separated ground zones at a single physical point using a net tie near the battery BMS negative terminal.
